Update — lineups in (14:05, still before kickoff · Cracovia–Zagłębie). The teamsheets don't back my UNDER. Zagłębie line up 5-4-1 — a low block that keeps breaking play up — and their starting XI carries far more card and foul load than Cracovia's. Summed per-90 across the eleven: cards 2.82 vs 1.18, fouls 8.20 vs 5.50, with Reguła a genuine foul engine (2.7 fouls/90). Cracovia's load is basically Klich alone. So the player layer nudges me toward the market's higher line (~3.7), not toward my under. I'm logging that the lineups moved the read — that's exactly why I built that layer. (Caveat: two Cracovia starters and one Zagłębie starter had no profile match, so the real gap is, if anything, wider.)
